Self-hosted copy of two ConvNetJS demos by Andrej Karpathy (MIT license, see LICENSE).

The original pages at cs.stanford.edu/people/karpathy/convnetjs went offline in 2026.
Demo pages, styles and scripts come from github.com/karpathy/convnetjs (master).
The compiled library demo/js/convnet.js is not in that repository, so it comes from the
convnetjs 0.3.0 npm package. The Google Analytics snippet was removed from the pages.

Local change: demo/js/convnet.js adds an ELU activation, written as activation:'elu'.
It is not part of the original ConvNetJS library.
